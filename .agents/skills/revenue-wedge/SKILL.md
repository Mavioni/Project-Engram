# Revenue Wedge

## Mandatory trigger
Use this skill for monetization, pricing, offer design, packaging, sales path, GTM, or first customer strategy.

## Contract
- Choose one wedge at a time.
- Prefer service revenue now; avoid forcing unfinished product subscription pressure.
- Define buyer, pain, offer, price anchor, channel, and proof metric.
- Keep claims testable.

## Workflow
1. Pick the first customer segment.
2. Name the urgent pain.
3. Define one scoped offer.
4. Set a price anchor and acquisition channel.
5. Define the next sales test.

## Output
- ICP
- Pain
- Offer
- Pricing
- Channel
- Proof metric
- Next test
