# Revenant AI Offer Stack

## Principle

Do not force Engram to make all the money before the product is ready. Use adjacent services to generate cash flow, prove demand, and feed the product roadmap.

## Engram paid product wedge

**Engram Pro: Private Weekly Review**

A paid layer that turns journal entries, check-ins, IRIS profile, and Engram progress into a weekly synthesis with patterns, blind spots, and one practical next action.

Initial price anchors:

- Self-serve: $4.99/month or $39/year.
- Concierge beta: higher-touch fixed fee while automation matures.

## Services Revenant AI can sell now

### 1. Private Self-Knowledge System Setup

A fixed-scope setup sprint for people who want a private reflection system but do not want to configure tools themselves.

Includes:

- Engram setup and onboarding.
- Initial IRIS walkthrough.
- Personal taxonomy for check-ins and journal tags.
- Data export/backup guidance.
- One follow-up review session.

### 2. AI Workflow Audit and Local-First Blueprint

A service for founders, creators, or small teams who want AI workflows without leaking sensitive context into a fragile SaaS stack.

Includes:

- Current workflow audit.
- Privacy and data-flow map.
- Local-first architecture proposal.
- Provider abstraction plan.
- Implementation backlog.

### 3. Concierge Pattern Review

A human-in-the-loop service using Engram as proof of capability.

Includes:

- Weekly review of entries and check-ins.
- Pattern summary.
- Behavioral themes.
- One focused recommendation.
- Optional setup of the next week's prompts.

## Packaging rules

- Every offer must be time-boxed.
- Every offer must preserve privacy-first positioning.
- Every offer should create reusable product insight.
- Avoid open-ended custom work unless priced as custom consulting.
- Do not sell vague transformation; sell a specific system or review.

## Recommended first sales motion

1. Sell 3 setup sprints to warm contacts.
2. Convert one into concierge review.
3. Use observed friction to improve onboarding and weekly review UX.
4. Turn the repeated service steps into Engram Pro features.

## Proof metrics

- Paid discovery calls booked.
- Setup sprint close rate.
- Time to deliver setup.
- Week-two retention after onboarding.
- Number of repeated service steps ready for automation.
