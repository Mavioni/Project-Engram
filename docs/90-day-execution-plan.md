# 90-Day Execution Plan

## Goal

Create a reliable engineering system, sharpen Engram's product wedge, and generate initial cash flow through Revenant AI services adjacent to Engram.

## Days 1-30: Stabilize and package

Deliverables:

- Root `AGENTS.md` and repo-local skills.
- Repo audit and agent architecture decision.
- Local AI plan and provider abstraction seam.
- One improved onboarding or demo-ready product surface.
- Three sellable service offer one-pagers.

Product focus:

- First-run clarity.
- Provider-neutral AI contract.
- Clear free vs paid promise.

Business focus:

- Define first buyer segment.
- Package setup sprint and concierge review.
- Build a short outreach list.

Success criteria:

- The repo is easier for Codex to operate.
- The product wedge can be explained in one sentence.
- First service offer can be sold without new product work.

## Days 31-60: Sell and learn

Deliverables:

- First paid setup or review engagement.
- Delivery checklist.
- Feedback log.
- Onboarding fixes from observed friction.
- Tests for AI fallback/provider behavior.

Product focus:

- Strengthen check-in -> journal -> dashboard -> insight flow.
- Improve demo readiness without backend configuration.
- Retire confusing legacy naming as touched.

Business focus:

- Run direct outreach.
- Offer limited setup sprint slots.
- Capture objections and repeated service steps.

Success criteria:

- At least one paid customer.
- One repeated service workflow identified for productization.
- Clear evidence on whether weekly review is the paid wedge.

## Days 61-90: Systemize and convert

Deliverables:

- Repeatable service delivery templates.
- Productized weekly review spec.
- Conversion path from service client to Engram Pro.
- Export/backup plan for local data.
- Local AI spike result with adopt/defer decision.

Product focus:

- Weekly review MVP.
- Provider routing plan for hosted/local/fallback.
- Stronger entitlement and subscription tests.

Business focus:

- Turn first customer proof into case notes.
- Refine pricing.
- Build referral loop.

Success criteria:

- Services are repeatable.
- Engram has a credible paid wedge.
- Local AI direction is validated before full integration.
- The next 90-day roadmap is based on real demand.
